---
name: Month-end inventory accuracy
description: Never substitute retrospectively reconstructed stock for an actual historical capture
---
Month-end inventory must be a dated capture of the recorded stock, not a reconstruction from today's watches. Leave missed months explicitly unavailable, and distinguish capture time from a guarantee of stock at the exact final instant of the month. Existing captures must stay unchanged when reports or watches change.

**Why:** Editable costs, statuses, asking prices and deleted watches cannot reliably reconstruct earlier holdings. Labelling those estimates as actual historical stock would mislead monthly business comparisons.

**How to apply:** Keep historical evidence separate from live inventory in both the notebook and AI analysis. If adding historical imports or corrections later, preserve provenance and the original evidence instead of silently overwriting a capture.
