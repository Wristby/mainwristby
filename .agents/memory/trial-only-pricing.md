---
name: Trial-only pricing
description: WRISTBY's confirmed trial and paid-subscription behavior.
---

New accounts receive a seven-day trial instead of a permanent Free plan. Full workspace and AI access starts when the user verifies their email. No payment details are required to start, and the trial never converts or charges automatically. At trial expiry, workspace access pauses until the user manually subscribes to Pro at €39.99 per month.

Existing accounts and the owner keep their prior access; the new trial policy applies to new accounts.

**Why:** the user chose a seven-day trial, no card up front, and manual subscription after the trial. Starting the clock after email verification avoids consuming trial time while a user is waiting for their verification email.

**How to apply:** Keep signup, trial status, access gates, billing checkout, and pricing copy consistent with this policy. Do not make the trial auto-renew or collect payment details before the user chooses to subscribe.
