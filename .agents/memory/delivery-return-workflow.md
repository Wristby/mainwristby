description: User's delivery confirmation choice and return-window purpose
---

The user wants to track a 14-day return-request window from customer delivery for watches sold in NL. They chose “Mark delivered manually” rather than automatic carrier lookup.

**Why:** They need an easy delivery confirmation and countdown to track when the customer can request a return.

**How to apply:** Keep delivery dates manually confirmable and correctable. Treat the countdown as an operational reminder, not a determination of legal eligibility.
